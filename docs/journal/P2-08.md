## Why / design decisions (and alternatives rejected)
- **Live Reactive Quote Calculation**: Rather than waiting for final booking submission to show the total price, `BookingModal` automatically invokes `/api/bookings/quote/` as dates, room count, or discount codes change. This gives the guest full transparency regarding nights, subtotal, discount reduction, and total price before committing.
- **Graceful Handling of HTTP 409 Conflict**: If two guests attempt to book the last available room concurrently, the backend responds with HTTP 409 Conflict. The frontend intercepts this specific status and renders an intuitive explanation in Persian («ظرفیت این نوع اتاق برای تاریخ‌های انتخابی تکمیل شده است») rather than a generic network error.
- **URL Parameter Driven Search State**: In `HotelsPage`, search parameters (`city`, `check_in`, `check_out`, `guests`, `min_price`, `max_price`, `q`) are kept in sync with the browser's URL search params. This enables deep-linking, bookmarking, and native back/forward browser navigation without losing filter state.
- **Client-Side Date and Capacity Guardrails**: While Django forms strictly validate all constraints on the backend, the React frontend prevents impossible states early (e.g. `min` date set to today, automatic increment of check-out when check-in is adjusted, and instant warning if `guests > rooms_count * capacity`).
- **Immediate Inventory Release on Cancellation**: When a guest cancels a future reservation from `/my-bookings`, the status updates locally to `cancelled` and the room inventory becomes immediately available for other guests on the search page.

## How it works (follow ONE request/click end to end)
1. **Search**:
   - Guest visits `/`, selects "تهران", dates `2026-12-01` to `2026-12-04`, 2 guests, and clicks "جستجوی هتل‌ها".
   - App navigates to `/hotels?city=تهران&check_in=2026-12-01&check_out=2026-12-04&guests=2`.
   - `HotelsPage` fetches matching hotels via `GET /api/hotels/?...`. Only hotels with available rooms are displayed.
2. **Hotel & Room Inspection**:
   - Guest clicks "مشاهده اتاق‌ها" on a hotel card.
   - App navigates to `/hotels/1?check_in=2026-12-01&check_out=2026-12-04&guests=2`.
   - `HotelDetailPage` loads hotel details and lists room types with real-time `available_count` badges and amenity tags.
3. **Booking & Price Quote**:
   - Guest clicks "رزرو این اتاق".
   - `BookingModal` opens with dates and room pre-populated.
   - Modal calls `POST /api/bookings/quote/` and displays: ۳ شب، مبلغ کل، تخفیف، و مبلغ قابل پرداخت.
   - Guest enters discount code `SUMMER20` and clicks "اعمال تخفیف". `POST /api/discounts/validate/` verifies the coupon and the quote updates reactively.
   - Guest clicks "تأیید و ثبت رزرو". `POST /api/bookings/` creates the confirmed reservation.
4. **My Bookings & Cancellation**:
   - On success, guest is redirected to `/my-bookings` with a green success message.
   - The new booking appears under "تأیید شده".
   - If plans change, the guest clicks "لغو این رزرو". A confirmation modal asks for confirmation, sends `POST /api/bookings/{id}/cancel/`, sets status to "لغو شده", and immediately frees the room inventory.
